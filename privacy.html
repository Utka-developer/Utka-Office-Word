Privacy Policy for Utka Office Word

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Utka Office Word ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Windows desktop application, including our integration with Google Drive services.

1. Accessing and Using Google Drive Data

Our application allows you to voluntarily connect your Google Drive account to open, edit, and save your documents.

  • Requested Scopes: We only request access to https://googleapis.com. This allows our application to read and write only the specific files and folders that you open or create using Utka Office Word.
  • Data Storage: We do not own, rent, or operate external servers to store your personal files or Google Drive data. All processing, OAuth authentication tokens, and file modifications happen locally on your Windows device.
  • No Data Transfer: We do not collect, view, store, or transmit your Google account passwords, files, or any confidential information. Your data remains strictly between your local device and Google’s official servers.

2. Google API Services Disclosure (Limited Use)

Utka Office Word’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google Drive data with any third parties, advertisers, or external entities.

3. License and Activation

Utka Office Word is free to use but requires a valid activation key upon the first launch for security and authorization purposes. Activation keys are processed strictly within the local client application.

4. No Online Features

Apart from the voluntary Google Drive integration, the application functions entirely offline as a local document editor. We do not provide real‑time co‑authoring, internal chats, or custom cloud synchronization.

5. User Control and Revocation

You can disconnect Google Drive from Utka Office Word at any time within the application settings. Additionally, you can completely revoke our access through your Google Security Settings page at: https://google.com.
